Play:   	Move your pieces by specifying the 'from' location and
		the 'to' location on the command line.
		Ex:  E2 E3 would move the pawn at grid point E2 to grid point E3.
		The game does not check for illegal moves, and will not designate
		a check or checkmate.  It is played against an opponent, and is
		meant to be played serially over a long period of time.
