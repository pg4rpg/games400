Name:		Slot Machine

Author:         Shankar Narayan (Shankar@aimcornet.com)

Objects:        SLOTDF		Display file
                SLOTPGM         RPG/400 Program

To Create:      Compile the display file SLOTDF first, and then RPG/400 program
		SLOTPGM.

Object:	        Win credits by matching like bars horizontally or diagonally.

To Start:	CALL SLOTPGM

Play:		Bet from 1-3 credits and press enter.  Watch the bars spin.
		Wheee|  As much fun as Vegas, but less costly.
